Our approach

Professional treatments. Personalized homecare.

At Fine Lines Aesthetic Salon, we believe that beautiful, healthy looking skin is achieved through the right combination of professional treatments and consistent homecare.

Every treatment is tailored to each client's skin's individual needs, while your homecare routine is carefully selected to support and maintain your results between appointments.

Treatments

Targeted treatments. Individual skin goals.

A treatment menu put together to address the skin concerns we see most often - with every treatment selected to suit your skin's individual needs.

Microneedling
Microneedling is a treatment that use small needles to create micro-channels in the skin, triggering your skin's natural healing response.
  • Boosts collagen and elastin
  • Reduces fine lines and wrinkles
  • Reduction of pore size
  • and so much more
Ask Michelle about this →
Chemical Peel
Gently exfoliates for a smoother, brighter, more even complexion: pigmentation, texture and breakouts.
  • Brightens and evens out skin tone
  • Softens the look of pigmentation
  • Refines texture and helps clear breakouts
Ask Michelle about this →
Dermaplaning
Dermaplane is a manual exfoliation procedure where a scalpel is used to gently scrape away the top layer of dead skin cells and fine vellus hair.
  • Gently exfoliates skin
  • Increase absorption of topical products
  • Creates healthier, smoother and more radiant skin
Ask Michelle about this →
Gel/ Cutex Pedicure
A Pedicure is a therapeutic treatment done for your feet that shapes your toenails and treat your feet.
  • Improves foot health
  • Enhance the appearance toenails and feet
  • Relaxes the body and feet

Questions, answered

A few helpful answers before you get in touch.

QWhat treatments do you currently offer?
Microneedling, chemical peels and dermaplaning are my main treatments, but I also offer pedicures and facial waxes and tints.
QHow often should I return for follow-up facial treatments?
The ideal treatment schedule depends on your skin and concerns. After each treatment, I will advise personalised advice based on the treatment performed and how your skin responds.
QI'm not sure which treatment to book. What should I do?
That's completely okay. Every skin is different and you don't have to know exactly what your skin needs before you book. Make your booking and I will recommend the treatment that is most suitable for your skin at the time of your appointment.
QDo I need to use Lamelle products to book a treatment?
No, not at all. Every client has the freedom to choose the skincare products they feel comfortable using, and I respect that. My role is to provide professional guidance and recommendations based on your individual skin needs, while always respecting your personal preferences.
QHow do I book an appointment?
Send me a WhatsApp. I will attend to your message as soon as possible.
